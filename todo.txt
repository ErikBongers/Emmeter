Rules of indent
================
* Equal indent can mean
  - plus sign
  - properties of element
    > extra indents of properties: the indent level can be ignored, as long as its equal or greater than the indent of the element.
* parenthesis completely reset the indent level.
* parser should re-use code as much as possible.
  - parsePlus:
    > loop all `+` signs, but also consider all equal (or higher) indents as plus signs.
    > even when using an indent, the plus sign can optionally be used.
      > so scan for
        - a plus and indent
        - or an indent and plus
